AI-Native Document Interaction Layer

Zoom out to compress a document into its gist, or select any line to rewrite it in place

Parser uses small and large language models in sequence, without ever leaving the page for a chat window.

01 — Core Feature
Zoom to summarize

Pinch or scroll out and the document itself compresses. Sentence to paragraph to single line to icon. Regenerated live by a small model first, then a large model as you pull further back. Zoom in and the original text returns exactly as written.

Zooming out escalates through model size only as compression deepens. Keeping the common case fast and cheap, reserving heavier reasoning for full-document synthesis.

Prior Art Note

The zoom-to-summarize concept is published academic prior art: Rambler (CHI 2024, UC Berkeley/Google) implements a semantic zoom slider with GPT-4 at four summarization levels. Sensecape (UIST 2023, UC San Diego) built multilevel LLM abstraction. Both cite the 1993 coinage of "semantic zoom" by Perlin & Fox. Parser's defensibility rests on execution, SLM/LLM tiering, and contract-vertical framing, not the core idea.
